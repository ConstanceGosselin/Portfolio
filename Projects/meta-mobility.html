Türkiye 2023 earthquake Master thesis

Analysis and validation of Meta Movement Data to investigate population mobility following the 6 February 2023 earthquake in Türkiye.

01

QUESTION

How can Meta Movement Data reveal population mobility following a major earthquake?

Major disasters can disrupt population mobility across space and time. This study investigated whether aggregated Meta Movement Data could capture these changes and provide useful information for analysing disaster-related mobility.

METHODOLOGY

From mobility observations to disaster analysis

Overall analytical workflow.

Analytical framework developed for the study.

Sensitivity analyses used to assess the robustness of the mobility observations.

The analysis combined temporal comparisons, spatial analysis and statistical modelling to assess mobility changes before and after the 2023 earthquake. 04

RESULTS

What the data reveal

04.1

Temporal patterns

Mobility patterns changed substantially following the earthquake, with distinct temporal responses across affected areas.

The temporal analysis was used to identify changes in mobility before and after the disaster and to examine how these responses evolved through time.

Temporal evolution of population mobility observed in the Meta Movement Data.

04.2

Validation and influencing factors

Differences in Meta Movement Data were associated with geographic and socio-demographic characteristics, highlighting important sources of spatial variation and potential bias.

The analysis examined relationships between differences in Meta Movement Data and selected geographic and socio-demographic variables to assess the sensitivity and representativeness of the data.

Relationships between differences in Meta Movement Data and selected contextual factors.

04.3

Earthquake-related mobility

The 6 February 2023 earthquake was associated with marked changes in population mobility, revealing a measurable disruption in movement patterns across affected areas.

Mobility changes were analysed in relation to the spatial distribution and intensity of the earthquake impacts to investigate differences in population response.

Population mobility before and after the 6 February 2023 earthquake.

05

KEY TAKEAWAYS

What this study shows

01

Disaster-related mobility can be observed through aggregated movement data. Meta Movement Data provide a dynamic perspective on changes in population movement following a major disaster.

02

Data representativeness varies across contexts. Geographic and socio-demographic characteristics can influence observed differences and should be considered when interpreting mobility indicators.

03

Mobility data can complement conventional disaster-impact information. Temporal and spatial mobility patterns provide an additional perspective for understanding population responses to disasters.
