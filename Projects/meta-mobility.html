Türkiye 2023 earthquake Master thesis

Analysis and validation of Meta Movement Data to investigate population mobility following the 6 February 2023 earthquake in Türkiye.

01

QUESTION

How can Meta Movement Data reveal population mobility following a major earthquake?

Major disasters can disrupt population mobility across space and time. This study investigated whether aggregated Meta Movement Data could capture these changes and provide useful information for analysing disaster-related mobility.

02

03

METHODOLOGY

From mobility observations to disaster analysis

Methodological workflow of the study. The methodology used to assess the impact of the 2023 earthquakes in Turkey on population mobility at both provincial and district levels. By leveraging Meta mobility data, correlation analyses, spatial Local Moran’s I, and linear regressions, the study evaluates pre- and post-disaster movement patterns across varying impact zones. It also highlights behavioral and resilience disparities between urban and rural areas relative to peak ground acceleration (PGA), contextualized through localized literature.
