DISASTER RISK · HUMAN MOBILITY

Disaster-Related
Population Mobility

Türkiye · 2023 earthquake · Master thesis

Analysis and validation of Meta Movement Data to investigate population mobility following the 6 February 2023 earthquake in Türkiye.

01

QUESTION

How can Meta Movement Data be used to analyse population mobility following a major earthquake?

Major disasters can disrupt population mobility at different spatial and temporal scales. This project investigated whether aggregated Meta Movement Data could capture these changes and provide useful information for analysing disaster-related mobility.

02

DATA

Data used in the analysis

The analysis combined aggregated mobility observations with geographic and contextual data to investigate population movement and assess the usability of Meta Movement Data.
